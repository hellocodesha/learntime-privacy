LearnTime Privacy Policy

Applies to LearnTime: Earn Screen Time (学后时光) · Last updated: November 8, 2026

LearnTime has no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics, no AI services, and no developer-operated server. Practice data and parent settings stay on your device. Family controls use Apple's system frameworks, and purchases are processed by Apple.

Information stored on the device

  • Practice activity. Practice dates, question counts, corrections, first answers used for review, earned and unspent minutes, the current play session, parent rules, and separate starting levels for math, reading, and logic.
  • Selected apps. Opaque tokens returned by Apple's family app picker. The app's own records do not contain the names of the apps a parent selects.
  • History limits. Up to the most recent 300 practice rounds in the current format, 300 legacy rounds, and up to 200 local control-diagnostic events.
  • Parent password and recovery code. Verification data is stored in the iOS Keychain. The app does not store the plaintext password or recovery code.
  • Learning content. The included stories and questions are bundled with the app.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or profile.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No developer server, cloud sync, or remote parent dashboard.
  • No reading of video content, browsing history, messages, photos, or contacts.
  • No audio recording. Read-aloud uses the system speech synthesizer and does not upload audio.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Apple system services

Family Controls and Screen Time. On a child device in Apple Family Sharing, a parent approves authorization. The app requests that authorization, lets the parent select individual entertainment apps, applies restrictions, and monitors usage thresholds. Apple handles the authorization flow and the system restriction interface.

StoreKit. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app receives the transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock purchased features. Payment details are handled by Apple, not by the developer.

Apple may process account, transaction, and diagnostic information under Apple's own privacy policy. This policy covers only the LearnTime app.

Purchases

Learning Plus is an optional auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Prices and billing periods are shown before purchase. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the Apple account settings. Restore Purchases is available in the parent area. A purchase is tied to the Apple account used to buy it.

Parent area and children's privacy

First-time setup, rule changes, purchases, external links, and record viewing are behind the parent area. The app does not ask for a child's name, contact details, or other profile information. Practice data is used only on the device to run learning rounds and show the parent's local summary. Parents can delete practice records in the parent area. Deleting records does not reset minutes already earned that day.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the app is deleted or a parent deletes practice records. Uninstalling the app removes its local practice data; Keychain items may remain across reinstalls. Because there is no developer account or server, there is no online account deletion process.

Diagnostics

The parent area can keep up to 200 local control events, including request or callback type, time, source, a random session identifier, allocated minutes, and whether a callback matched the current session. A parent can export these events manually. Exported diagnostics do not include selected app names or tokens, question answers, or child identity. The app does not upload exports automatically. An exported file may reveal device usage times, so share it only with someone you trust. Diagnostic events do not by themselves prove that a restriction was enforced.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, or other data processing, this policy and the relevant permissions will be updated before that version is released. The current version will always be posted at this URL.

学后时光隐私政策

适用于 LearnTime: Earn Screen Time(学后时光)· 更新日期:2026 年 11 月 8 日

学后时光没有账户、广告、第三方统计、AI 服务或开发者服务器。练习数据和家长设置保存在设备上。设备限制依赖 Apple 系统功能,购买由 Apple 处理。

保存在设备上的信息

  • 练习记录。 练习日期、题数、订正次数、用于复习的首次作答、已赚取和未使用分钟、当前娱乐时段、家长规则,以及数学、阅读、逻辑各自的起点。
  • 所选 App。 Apple 家庭 App 选择器返回的不可读标记。本 App 自己的记录不包含家长所选 App 的名称。
  • 记录上限。 新格式练习记录和旧格式记录各保留最近 300 轮,本地管控诊断最多保留 200 条。
  • 家长密码和恢复码。 校验信息保存在系统钥匙串。App 不保存密码和恢复码原文。
  • 学习内容。 内置短文和题目随 App 一起安装。

App 不会做的事

  • 不需要账户、登录或资料。
  • 不包含广告或第三方统计 SDK。
  • 没有开发者服务器、云同步或家长手机远程管理。
  • 不读取视频内容、浏览历史、信息、照片或联系人。
  • 不录音。朗读使用系统语音合成,不会上传音频。
  • 不出售或共享个人数据。

Apple 系统服务

Family Controls 与屏幕使用时间。 在 Apple 家庭共享的儿童设备上,由家长批准授权。App 请求该授权,让家长逐个选择娱乐 App,应用限制并监测使用阈值。授权流程和系统限制界面由 Apple 提供。

StoreKit。 购买和订阅由 Apple 处理。App 只接收解锁已购功能所需交易与权益信息。付款信息由 Apple 处理,开发者无法获取。

Apple 可能依据其自身隐私政策处理账户、交易和诊断信息。本政策仅适用于学后时光 App。

购买

学习进阶为可选的月度/年度自动续订或一次性购买。购买前会显示价格和计费周期。订阅可在 Apple 账户设置中管理或取消。家长区提供恢复购买。购买与用于购买的 Apple 账户绑定。

家长区与儿童隐私

首次设置、修改规则、购买、外链和查看记录都在家长区之后。App 不要求输入孩子姓名、联系方式或其他资料。练习数据只在设备上用于完成练习轮次和生成本地家长摘要。家长可以在家长区删除练习记录;删除记录不会重置当天已赚取的额度。

保留与删除

数据保存在设备上,直到删除 App 或由家长删除练习记录。卸载 App 会删除本地练习数据;钥匙串信息可能跨重装保留。由于没有开发者账户或服务器,不存在在线账户删除流程。

本地管控诊断

家长区最多保留 200 条本地管控事件,包括请求或回调类型、时间、来源、随机时段标识、分配分钟,以及回调是否匹配当前时段。家长可以手动导出。导出内容不包含所选 App 名称或标记、题目答案或孩子身份,App 不会自动上传。导出文件可能反映设备使用时段,请只分享给信任的接收者。诊断事件本身不能证明限制实际生效。

变更

如果未来版本增加云同步、统计或其他数据处理,会在该版本发布前更新本政策和相关权限说明。最新版本始终发布在本网址。
